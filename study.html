    Phase by phase

    Phase 1 · Foundation sprint

    Aug 11 – Sep 21 · 6 weeks

    Build the entire N2 vocabulary and kanji base from a standing start, while introducing grammar systematically. Reading stays at N3 level so comprehension doesn't stall while vocab ramps up.

    Anki~20–25 new words/day + reviews
    Kanji Dojo~8–10 new kanji/day + reviews
    Renshuu~2–3 new grammar points/day
    ReadingN3-level, comprehension-focused
    ImmersionCasual, JP subtitles, no pressure
    MigiiNone — one baseline diagnostic in week 1

    Phase 2 · Build-up

    Sep 22 – Oct 26 · 5 weeks

    Finish new vocabulary and grammar intake, move reading up to N2-level material, and start weekly mock testing. Immersion becomes more structured.

    Anki~15–18 new words/day, tapering
    Kanji DojoMostly review, clear stragglers early
    RenshuuFinish grammar list, drill confusable pairs
    ReadingN2-level short/medium passages, timed
    ImmersionYouTube/podcasts + weekend films, JP subs
    MigiiWeekly sectional mock, Sundays

    Phase 3 · Exam simulation

    Oct 27 – Nov 30 · 5 weeks

    Stop adding new material — this phase is pure reinforcement and exam conditioning. Reading moves to full timed passages, immersion shifts toward listening without subtitles, and mock tests go full-length.

    AnkiReviews only, clear leeches
    Kanji DojoReviews only
    RenshuuTargeted review of weak points
    ReadingFull N2 section, timed, 2–3x/week
    ImmersionPodcasts/dramas w/o subtitles — listening prep
    MigiiFull mock weekly + Monday error review

    Phase 4 · Taper

    Dec 1 – Dec 6 · exam week

    Protect what you've built. No new material of any kind — just light review and rest.

    Tue Dec 1Last full mock, review lightly
    Wed–Fri Dec 2–4Skim mistake notebook, light reviews only
    Sat Dec 5Very light review, prep materials, early night
    Sun Dec 6Exam day
    • Immersion time doubles as listening prep for 聴解 — by phase 3, favor unsubtitled audio over subtitled video.
    • The daily new-item paces above are ballpark figures, not official counts — JLPT hasn't published exact vocab/kanji lists since the 2010 revision. Adjust to match your actual Anki deck and Kanji Dojo course size.
    • Missed a day? Fold it into the weekend block rather than doubling up the next day — consistency beats cramming.
